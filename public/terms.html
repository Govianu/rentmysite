1. Who we are

RentMySite.ai (“RentMySite”, “we”, “us”) is a Govianu venture based in Bengaluru, India. We connect businesses (“hosts”), professionals, and brands or suppliers (“partners”) so they can earn from space, hours and customers that already exist, under written agreements and transparent revenue sharing.

After you agree to these terms, we email you a one-time verification code to open your dashboard. Your profile stays “pending” until our team has reviewed it. Keep sign-in codes private; you are responsible for activity under your account. Tell us straight away at info@rentmysite.ai if you think someone else has used it.

Sending an enquiry does not by itself create a partnership or any obligation to transact. Commercial commitments begin only when an agreement or a booking is accepted as described below.

3. Agreements for each collaboration

Partnership in principle. When you agree to these terms you join RentMySite as a partner in principle. This lets us plan collaborations, group deals and orders around your business. It does not by itself commit you to any collaboration, order or payment.

Group deals. You authorise us to include your business when we negotiate group deals on behalf of many partners, for example bulk supplies, brand placements or merchandise in your outlet, and to present your business to suppliers and brands for that purpose. We never place an order or commit you to a deal without your confirmation.

No direct contact. RentMySite is the only point of contact for every collaboration, order and booking. We never share your phone number, email or address with buyers, customers, brands, professionals or other businesses, and we never share theirs with you. All communication, orders, bookings and payments go only through RentMySite and our central billing desk.

Each host and each partner signs one master agreement with us, plus a one-page opportunity sheet for every collaboration. The sheet sets out the percentages, fees, who funds stock, equipment, marketing and staff, and who is responsible for ownership, installation, maintenance, damage, insurance and exit. If anything in a signed sheet conflicts with these terms, the sheet wins for that collaboration.

4. Revenue sharing and payments

We never split gross sales. For every payment, taxes come off first, then refunds and cancellations, then payment and booking charges, then the direct costs agreed in advance. What remains is shared between host, partner and platform at the percentages agreed in writing, as a share of net profit, decided case by case (often 50-50).

Equipment and fit-out funding. Where equipment or a fit-out is paid for by RentMySite, a bank, an investor or another party, the opportunity sheet (a three-party agreement where a lender or investor is involved) sets a fixed share of net collections that goes to repaying that investment first, for example 20%, until it is repaid. Profit is shared only after that.

Payments are digital only, through QR codes, booking links or point-of-sale integrations, and may be split automatically through our payment provider. Every party sees the same statement of sales, taxes, refunds, costs, shares and settlement status. You are responsible for your own taxes on amounts you receive.

5. Bookings

Customers and event companies can send booking requests for a host’s space or services. When the host ticks the partner agreement and presses “Accept” on their dashboard, the request becomes a match. Our team then approves it, our share is settled, and the booking is confirmed. Contact details are never shared between the host and the customer: our team coordinates everything. Accepting means agreeing to host it on the revenue split shown for that booking and on the terms of the relevant opportunity sheet. Hosts may decline any request. Cancellations and refunds follow the policy in the opportunity sheet.
