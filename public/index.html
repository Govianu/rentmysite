      The mandate, in plain English

      One agreement. We do the rest.

      A summary of what the owner agreement says. The signed mandate governs; this is not a substitute for it.

      What you grant

      The exclusive right, for the term, to find, contract and place brands and operators on your dormant space, and to sub-license space to them. No lease is created and no ownership passes.

      What you receive

      No rent. A share of site profit, 50% in the standard tier, paid monthly within ten days of month end. One audit of the site accounts each year, at your request.

      Your share, by who funds the build

      You fund it: 65%, and the assets are yours. The brand funds it: 50% (standard). We fund it: 35%, recovered from revenue over the term, nothing out of your pocket.

      Build-out and assets

      Assets belong to whoever paid for them, and are removed or handed over at the end of the term.

      Approvals

      You approve the site plan and operator mix before anything is built. We obtain and pay for operating permits and licences.

      Insurance

      We hold public liability cover for the site, and every operator insures its own activity.

      Your undertakings

      Clear title, site access, and no parallel arrangements on the space during the term. Taxes on the land stay with you.

      Selling the land

      Sell at any time. The buyer steps into the mandate, or the site winds down on notice with the unrecovered build cost settled as the schedule sets out.

      Ending it, and disputes

      Either side may end the mandate for a material breach not cured within thirty days. Force majeure suspends obligations while it lasts. Disputes go to arbitration in Bengaluru under Indian law.

      Questions owners ask

      Straight answers.

      Is this a lease?

      No. The mandate gives us the right to place operators and license space. No tenancy is created and you keep the title.

      Do I lose control of my property?

      No. You decide who takes it, what it is used for, for how long and during which hours. You approve the plan before anything is built.

      How much will I earn?

      It depends on location, size and local demand. Your free report gives an indicative range, and a survey confirms it. Income is not promised or guaranteed.

      What is the shortest period?

      For spaces rented to a business, two months. Most bookings run from 2 to 11 months and can be renewed. Operated multi-use sites run on a longer mandate.

      What if I want to sell?

      Sell any time. The buyer steps into the mandate, or the site winds down on notice as the schedule sets out.

      I'm an NRI. Can I take part?

      Yes, on the same terms, signed through a registered power of attorney, with income paid to your NRO account.

      I am a tenant. Can I list?

      Yes, if your landlord agrees in writing.

      Who pays for permits, insurance and staff?

      We do. Taxes on the land stay with the owner, as with any tenant.

      What if a brand stops paying, or leaves?

      Brands pay us, not you. We manage collections, hold deposits and replace operators who fall behind from our bundle pipeline. A multi-brand site keeps earning while a slot is refilled.

      Who insures the site?

      We do, for public liability and operator activity. The mandate lists the cover and the insurer.

      Can I use part of the space myself?

      Yes, if we agree it in the plan before build-out.

      Is there a listing fee?

      No fee and no obligation until you sign the mandate. The estimate and the survey are free.

      What about property tax?

      Taxes on the land remain yours. Operating permits and licences for the uses are ours to obtain and pay for.

      How are the estimates made?

      From more than 1,000 site assessments, by use, city, frontage, surroundings and surface. A survey confirms the number before a mandate proposal.

      How is this different from a broker?

      A broker finds one long-term tenant for the whole property. We find users for the parts and hours that sit idle, run the site, and handle the paperwork and collections.
