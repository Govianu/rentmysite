Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Is this a lease?

No. The mandate gives us the right to place operators and license space. No tenancy is created and you keep the title.

Do I lose control of my property?

No. You decide who takes it, what it is used for, for how long and during which hours. You approve the plan before anything is built.

How much will I earn?

It depends on location, size and local demand. Your free report gives an indicative range, and a survey confirms it. Income is not promised or guaranteed.

What is the shortest period?

For spaces rented to a business, two months. Most bookings run from 2 to 11 months and can be renewed. Operated multi-use sites run on a longer mandate.

What if I want to sell?

Sell any time. The buyer steps into the mandate, or the site winds down on notice as the schedule sets out.

I'm an NRI. Can I take part?

Yes, on the same terms, signed through a registered power of attorney, with income paid to your NRO account.

I am a tenant. Can I list?

Yes, if your landlord agrees in writing.

Who pays for permits, insurance and staff?

We do. Taxes on the land stay with the owner, as with any tenant.

How is this different from a broker?

A broker finds one long-term tenant for the whole property. We find users for the parts and hours that sit idle, run the site, and handle the paperwork and collections.
